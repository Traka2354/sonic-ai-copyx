What is the minimum deposit?

Are my funds safe?

Your funds are held in your own personal Tag Markets trading account — fully regulated. We do not hold, pool, or have access to your capital. You can withdraw at any time.

Is copy trading legal?

Yes. Copy trading is a fully legal, regulated financial service. Tag Markets is a licensed broker, and copy trading is an accepted form of social investing used by millions of retail investors worldwide. All trades are executed on a regulated platform and are fully transparent.

What is the difference between 12X and 24X?

Both accounts copy the same gold trading strategy. The difference is the leverage multiplier applied to your deposit. 12X is lower risk with moderate weekly returns; 24X offers higher potential returns with commensurately higher risk. We recommend 24X only for investors comfortable with higher volatility.

Can I withdraw my profits at any time?

Yes. Your profits and capital sit in your own Tag Markets account. You can request a withdrawal at any time through the Tag Markets platform — no lock-up periods, no exit penalties.
